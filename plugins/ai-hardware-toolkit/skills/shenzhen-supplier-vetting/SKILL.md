# Shenzhen supplier vetting

The red flags, green flags and scorecard are in [reference/supplier-red-flags.md](reference/supplier-red-flags.md).

## How to use it

1. **Desktop stage**: check the supplier against the online red flags first. Mark each as CLEAR, FLAGGED or UNKNOWN, with what you looked at (business registration, website, photos, references). UNKNOWN means not checked yet, not clear.
2. **Visit or audit stage**: turn the production, component-sourcing and quality red flags into a visit checklist: what to look at, what to ask, what document to request.
3. **Negotiation stage**: check the quote and contract terms against the business red flags, especially mold ownership, payment terms and an NNN agreement.
4. **Score** with the weighted scorecard and compare suppliers on the same sheet.
5. **Stay factual**: report what the evidence shows. A red flag is a reason to ask a question, not proof of bad faith.

## Output format

Per supplier: a table of red flag, status, evidence; the scorecard total; and the open questions to resolve before an order.
