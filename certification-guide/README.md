# Certification Guide for AI Hardware

Which regulatory certifications you need, what they cost, and how to avoid the mistakes that delay most first-time hardware companies by 2-3 months.

## What's Inside

**[certification-decision-tree.md](./certification-decision-tree.md)** -- A 3-step decision framework:

1. **Identify target markets** -- Market-to-certification mapping table
2. **Understand each certification** -- 8 certifications covered in detail:
   - FCC (US) -- $3K-10K, 4-8 weeks
   - CE/RED (EU) -- $3K-12K, 6-10 weeks
   - UL/IEC 62368 (Global Safety) -- $5K-20K, 8-14 weeks
   - SRRC (China) -- $4K-15K, 6-10 weeks
   - CCC (China) -- $5K-20K, 8-16 weeks
   - MIC/TELEC (Japan) -- $3K-10K, 4-8 weeks
   - ISED (Canada) -- $2K-8K, 4-6 weeks
   - UN38.3 (Battery Transport) -- $1.5K-5K, 3-6 weeks
3. **Plan your strategy** -- Budget templates and money-saving tactics

Each certification includes: requirements, cost range, timeline, specific test lab recommendations in Shenzhen, and tips from real hardware projects.

## The Most Expensive Mistakes

The guide covers 6 common mistakes, but these two cost the most:

1. **Certifying before design freeze.** Any PCB change after certification means re-testing ($3K-5K+ and 4-8 weeks lost).
2. **Forgetting UN38.3.** Without it, your freight forwarder will refuse to ship your batteries. Your entire supply chain stops.
